#+title: Well pump

Submersible pump, Grundfos SQ 2-85, half horsepower, 230 volts, set at 180 feet.
Installed in 2019. The pressure switch is set to 40/60 psi (cut in at 40, cut
out at 60). The pressure tank is a 44 gallon bladder tank in the basement,
precharged to 38 psi.

* Maintenance
- Check the tank precharge every spring with the power off and the tank drained.
- The breaker is number 14 in the main panel.
